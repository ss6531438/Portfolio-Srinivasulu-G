I’m happy to share that I participated in the 24-hour Advaya 2.0 Hackathon, organized by BGS College of Engineering and Technology (BGSCET) on April 1st and 2nd, 2026.

Along with my team ENCRYPTED EMPIRES, and teammates Yogesh Naik and Mayur M, we presented our project: 🌾 AgriTrust – Smart Crop Transparency & Supply Chain Traceability Platform.

AgriTrust is a full-stack web application designed to improve transparency between farmers, distributors, and consumers through QR-based crop lifecycle tracking.

🔍 Key Features of AgriTrust:

  • ✓Crop lifecycle tracking from farm to consumer
  • ✓Fertilizer, irrigation & pesticide usage monitoring
  • ✓Transparency score generation (0–100) based on cultivation practices
  • ✓Distributor logistics tracking (warehouse, temperature, transport mode)
  • ✓QR-based crop verification system for consumers
  • ✓Smart fertilizer recommendation support
  • ✓Crop market price estimation for farmers

🌐 Inclusive & Farmer-Friendly Design:

  • •Multi-language support: Available across regional languages for seamless accessibility
  • •Voice input feature: Empowers farmers to operate the system effortlessly without literacy barriers
  • •Icon-driven & colorful UI: Optimized for rapid visual clarity and usability in rural contexts

“Participating in this 24-hour hackathon was a great learning experience that strengthened our team collaboration, problem-solving skills, and full-stack development experience while working on a real-world agriculture transparency solution.”

I recently had the opportunity to interact with the first-year CSE–A students of BGSCET, where I conducted an engaging session on core AI search algorithms — BFS, DFS, DLS, and IDDFS.

Instead of relying on a traditional static PowerPoint presentation, I developed and leveraged interactive web applications that enabled students to input custom nodes, connect edges dynamically, and visualize the step-by-step internal working flow of each algorithm in real-time.

This hands-on, practical approach helped them intuitively grasp the underlying data structures (queues, stacks, visited sets), real-time applications, algorithmic pseudocode, and performance measures.

“This session was impactful in two ways: it allowed me to share practical engineering knowledge with juniors while also enhancing my communication, presentation, and pedagogical skills.”
